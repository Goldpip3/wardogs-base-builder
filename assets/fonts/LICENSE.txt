Latin-subset woff2 at 400, 600 and 700, embedded as base64 in the planner so it
keeps working with no network. The site pages load the same files from /fonts/.

One family sets the whole site. Inter was the display face until 2026-08-31 and
Barlow set everything else until the same day; two families read as two sites, so
both were dropped rather than left loading.
