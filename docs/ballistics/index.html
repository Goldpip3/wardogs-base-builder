Not solved yet

Two things on this page are missing on purpose

  • Range falloff. Damage clearly drops with distance and slower rounds drop faster, but no published table separates falloff from the other factors, so every figure here is point blank.
  • Zone multipliers outside the torso are measured off the 5.56 assault rifle profile. The head, neck and limb ratios hold across four weapons; the torso ratio does not, and varies by up to a fifth. Non-torso figures for heavier calibres are indicative until launch.

Health is 100. Per-weapon damage is solved back out of published shots-to-kill figures rather than copied, which means it arrives with error bars: hover a damage figure to see the interval it came from. The full working, and the two checks it has to pass, are written up in docs/ballistics-sources.md in the repository.
