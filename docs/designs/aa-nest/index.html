Why it is built this way

The Vanguard CIWS needs clear sky and a gunner who is not immediately shot. Low Hesco alone leaves you exposed; tall Hesco blocks the gun. Stacking sandbags on top of low Hesco lands in between, and gives you peek-downs on the approach.

Keep the sky above it clear — anything built over an emplacement blocks it, and the planner will flag that.
