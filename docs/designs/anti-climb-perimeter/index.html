Why it is built this way

A perimeter made of waist-height blocks is a speed bump — infantry vault it. This run uses Hesco Wall (Quad) sections at full height with Bremer walls capped on top.

Bremer walls are the finishing layer specifically because nothing can be built on top of one. Put them down last, and put them on top of the Hesco rather than on the ground where they just become an expensive short wall.
